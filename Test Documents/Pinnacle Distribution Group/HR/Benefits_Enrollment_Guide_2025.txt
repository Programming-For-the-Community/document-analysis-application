WHAT'S CHANGING FOR 2025

- MEDICAL: Anthem BlueCross premiums increasing approximately 4.2%.
  Pinnacle will absorb 60% of the increase; employee portion increases as noted below.
- DENTAL: No change in plan design or premiums.
- VISION: New carrier effective January 1 — EyeMed replaces VSP. Network
  is comparable; vision benefits are otherwise unchanged.
- HSA: IRS maximum contribution limits for 2025 are $4,300 (individual),
  $8,550 (family). Pinnacle seed contribution remains $500 for individual,
  $1,000 for family.

401(k) — FIDELITY

Pinnacle matches 50% of your contribution up to 6% of eligible compensation.
To receive the full match, contribute at least 6%.

Example: Salary $52,000 → Max match-eligible contribution = $3,120 → Pinnacle adds $1,560.

Enrollment and changes at netbenefits.fidelity.com. Contact HR for your plan code.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LIFE INSURANCE

Pinnacle provides company-paid basic life insurance equal to 1× annual salary
(rounded to next $10,000, max $200,000) at no cost to you.

Supplemental life available: up to 5× salary, employee-paid. Evidence of
insurability required for amounts over $100,000.
