WELCOME

Welcome to Pinnacle Distribution Group. We are a regional leader in third-party
logistics, warehousing, and last-mile delivery serving clients across the Midwest.
This handbook describes your rights, responsibilities, and the standards we hold
ourselves to every day. Please sign the acknowledgment at the back and return it
to HR within your first week.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 1 — EMPLOYMENT POLICIES

1.1  Equal Opportunity
Pinnacle Distribution Group is an equal opportunity employer committed to a workplace
free from discrimination or harassment of any kind.

1.2  Classification
Employees are classified as Full-Time (35+ hours/week), Part-Time (<35 hours/week),
or Temporary. Benefits eligibility varies by classification; see Section 4.

1.3  Work Schedules
Operations run 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Schedules are assigned by department:
  - Warehouse Associates: rotating shifts (A/B/C), 10-hour days, 4 days on/3 off
  - Office and Management: Monday–Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
  - Drivers: per DOT hours-of-service regulations

1.4  Remote Work
Warehouse, operations, and driver roles are on-site only. Administrative roles
may be eligible for up to 2 days of remote work per week after 90 days, with
manager approval.

1.5  Attendance
All absences must be reported to the direct supervisor at least two hours before
the scheduled shift. Three unexcused absences in a rolling 90-day window will
result in formal disciplinary action.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 2 — CODE OF CONDUCT

2.1  Professionalism
Employees represent Pinnacle Distribution Group to our clients and the public.
All interactions — in person, by phone, or electronically — must be professional
and respectful.

2.2  Social Media
Employees must not post confidential client information, internal operational
details, or content that could damage Pinnacle's reputation on any social platform.

2.3  Conflicts of Interest
Employees may not work for, invest in, or hold ownership in a competing logistics
or warehousing company without prior written disclosure to HR.

2.4  Electronic Systems
Company computers, phones, and systems are for business use. Pinnacle reserves the
right to monitor usage. Personal use should be limited and must not interfere with work.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 3 — COMPENSATION

3.1  Pay Schedule
All employees are paid weekly on Thursdays. Pay stubs are available via the
Paylocity employee portal.

3.2  Overtime
Non-exempt employees are compensated at 1.5× for hours over 40 in a workweek.
Drivers are compensated per DOT regulations and applicable state law.

3.3  Mileage and Expense Reimbursement
Employees using personal vehicles for approved business travel are reimbursed at
the current IRS standard mileage rate. Expense reports must be submitted within
30 days of the expense.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4 — BENEFITS

4.1  Health Insurance
Full-time employees are eligible for medical, dental, and vision after 60 days.
Pinnacle covers 75% of the employee-only premium. Family plans available.

4.2  401(k) Plan
Eligible after 6 months. Pinnacle matches 50% of contributions up to 6% of
eligible compensation. Five-year graded vesting schedule.

4.3  PTO
  - Years 0–1:    5 days (prorated)
  - Years 1–3:    10 days
  - Years 4–6:    15 days
  - Years 7+:     20 days

Unused PTO up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year.

4.4  Paid Holidays
New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day,
Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Day.

4.5  CDL License Support
Pinnacle will reimburse up to $3,500 of CDL licensing costs for employees in
eligible driver-track roles, subject to a 2-year service commitment.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5 — SAFETY AND DOT COMPLIANCE

5.1  DOT Regulations
All Class A and Class B CDL drivers are subject to Federal Motor Carrier Safety
Administration (FMCSA) regulations including hours-of-service, pre/post-trip
inspections, drug and alcohol testing, and medical certification requirements.

5.2  Accident Reporting
Any vehicle accident — regardless of fault — must be reported to a supervisor and
the Safety Manager immediately. A written incident report must be filed within 24 hours.

5.3  Warehouse Safety
Forklift and reach truck operators must hold current internal certification.
Speed limits (3 mph pedestrian zones, 5 mph travel lanes) must be observed.
PPE (safety vests, steel-toed footwear) is mandatory in all warehouse areas.
