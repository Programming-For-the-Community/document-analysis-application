POSITION SUMMARY

The Warehouse Supervisor oversees daily warehouse operations including receiving,
storage, order fulfillment, and outbound shipping. This role is responsible for
leading a team of 8–12 warehouse associates, ensuring accurate inventory records,
and maintaining compliance with safety and quality standards.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

- Plan, coordinate, and supervise daily warehouse activities including inbound
  receiving, put-away, picking, packing, and shipping
- Lead, coach, and develop a team of warehouse associates; conduct performance
  reviews and provide ongoing feedback
- Ensure inventory accuracy through cycle counts and annual physical inventory;
  investigate and resolve discrepancies
- Maintain compliance with all OSHA safety regulations; enforce PPE usage and
  safe work practices at all times
- Coordinate with the production scheduler and customer service team to meet
  outbound shipping commitments
- Operate and oversee the use of forklifts, pallet jacks, and other material
  handling equipment; ensure all operators are certified
- Identify and implement process improvements to increase throughput and reduce
  error rates
- Maintain accurate records in the ERP system (Epicor); generate daily and weekly
  operational reports for management

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
QUALIFICATIONS

Required:
  - High school diploma or GED; associate's or bachelor's degree preferred
  - Minimum 3 years of warehouse or distribution experience
  - Minimum 1 year of supervisory or lead experience
  - Forklift certification (or ability to obtain within 30 days of hire)
  - Proficiency with warehouse management systems or ERP software
  - Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
  - Ability to lift up to 50 lbs and stand for extended periods

Preferred:
  - Experience with Epicor or similar manufacturing ERP
  - Familiarity with ISO 9001 quality management requirements
  - APICS CPIM or similar supply chain certification
