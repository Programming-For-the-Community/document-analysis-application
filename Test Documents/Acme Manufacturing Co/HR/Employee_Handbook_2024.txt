WELCOME

Welcome to Acme Manufacturing Co. This handbook outlines the policies, expectations,
and benefits that govern your employment. Please read it carefully and keep a copy
for your reference. Questions should be directed to the HR department.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 1 — EMPLOYMENT POLICIES

1.1  Equal Opportunity
Acme Manufacturing Co. is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on
the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or any
other characteristic protected by law.

1.2  At-Will Employment
Unless otherwise stated in a written contract, all employment is at-will and may be
terminated by either party at any time for any lawful reason.

1.3  Work Hours
Standard work hours are Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM – 3:30 PM (production floor)
and 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (office staff). Overtime must be approved in writing by a
supervisor prior to the work being performed.

1.4  Attendance and Punctuality
Employees are expected to arrive on time and ready to work. Unexcused absences or
repeated tardiness may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.
Employees must notify their supervisor at least one hour before their shift begins
if they will be absent.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 2 — CODE OF CONDUCT

2.1  Professional Behavior
All employees are expected to treat coworkers, customers, and vendors with respect.
Harassment, bullying, or intimidation of any kind will not be tolerated.

2.2  Confidentiality
Employees may not share proprietary information, trade secrets, customer data, or
financial details with unauthorized parties during or after employment.

2.3  Use of Company Property
Company equipment, vehicles, and facilities are for business use only. Personal use
requires prior written approval from a manager.

2.4  Drug and Alcohol Policy
Acme Manufacturing maintains a drug- and alcohol-free workplace. Testing may be
required pre-employment, post-accident, or when reasonable suspicion exists.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 3 — COMPENSATION AND PAYROLL

3.1  Pay Periods
Employees are paid bi-weekly on Fridays. Pay stubs are available electronically via
the ADP employee portal.

3.2  Overtime
Non-exempt employees are eligible for overtime pay at 1.5× their regular rate for
hours worked in excess of 40 in a workweek, per the Fair Labor Standards Act.

3.3  Performance Reviews and Merit Increases
Formal performance reviews are conducted annually in December. Merit increases, if
applicable, take effect on January 1 of the following year.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 4 — BENEFITS

4.1  Health Insurance
Full-time employees are eligible for medical, dental, and vision coverage effective
the first day of the month following 30 days of employment. Acme covers 80% of the
employee-only premium. Dependents may be added at the employee's expense.

4.2  401(k) Retirement Plan
Employees may contribute to the company 401(k) plan starting after 90 days of
employment. Acme matches 100% of contributions up to 4% of eligible compensation.
Employees are fully vested after three years of service.

4.3  Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue PTO at the following rates:
  - Years 0–2:    10 days/year
  - Years 3–5:    15 days/year
  - Years 6+:     20 days/year

4.4  Holidays
Acme observes the following paid holidays:
  New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day,
  Thanksgiving Day, Day after Thanksgiving, Christmas Eve, Christmas Day.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
SECTION 5 — SAFETY

5.1  Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
All production floor employees are required to wear appropriate PPE at all times,
including steel-toed boots, safety glasses, and hearing protection in designated areas.

5.2  Incident Reporting
Any workplace injury, near-miss, or unsafe condition must be reported to a supervisor
and the Safety Officer immediately. Failure to report incidents is a policy violation.

5.3  Emergency Procedures
Emergency evacuation routes are posted at each exit. Assembly points are in the
east parking lot (Building A) and the north lot (Building B). Fire extinguishers
are inspected monthly.
