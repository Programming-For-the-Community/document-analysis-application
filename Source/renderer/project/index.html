Layout Options
ClassicBalanced force-directed layout. Nodes repel each other while edges act as springs, producing natural clusters.
ForceFaster force-directed algorithm (fcose). Better suited to large graphs with many nodes.
RadialPlaces a root node at the center with connected nodes radiating outward in rings.
TreeTop-down breadth-first tree. Good for showing parent-child or hierarchical relationships.
HierarchicalLayered rank-based layout (dagre). Ideal for directed graphs and workflow diagrams.
ConcentricMost-connected nodes placed at the center, with less-connected nodes on outer rings.
CircleAll nodes evenly spaced on a single circle. Simple and easy to read for smaller graphs.
GridNodes arranged in a uniform rectangular grid. Useful for comparing many nodes side-by-side.
